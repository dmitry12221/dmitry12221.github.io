Welcome

Welcome to the Quantum Design Group at Skoltech. Our research combines theory and computation to understand and design electronic and optical phenomena in moiré superlattices. We develop tools that connect microscopic structure with measurable quantum behaviour, and we use them to explore new electronic phases, collective states, and the building blocks of quantum technologies.

What we study

Moiré superlattices are formed when two crystalline layers are stacked with a small relative twist or lattice mismatch. The resulting long-period pattern reshapes the motion and interaction of electrons.

Our work develops theoretical and computational tools to connect microscopic structure with measurable quantum behaviour.

Excitonic physics

Excitons and trions in two-dimensional semiconductors and heterostructures.

Moiré heterostructures

Strain-controlled atomic reconstruction and quasi-1D excitons in twisted systems.

Quantum emitters

Deterministic single-photon sources in atomically thin materials.
