Research

What we work on

Excitonic physics of two-dimensional materials: moiré heterostructures, quantum emitters, and light–matter coupling, explored through theory and experiment.

Excitons in two-dimensional semiconductors

Theory of neutral and charged excitons in van der Waals heterostructures, including magnetic semiconductors such as CrSBr. Binding energies, radiative decay, and optical response.

Excitons · Trions · Magnetic semiconductors

Moiré heterostructures

Strain-controlled atomic reconstruction and quasi-1D excitons in moiré superlattices. How local relaxation reshapes electronic and excitonic properties.

Moiré superlattices · Strain · Reconstruction

Quantum emitters

Deterministic single-photon sources in atomically thin materials. Positioning of molecules on TMD monolayers and control of localized exciton emission.

Single-photon sources · TMDs · Nano-optics

Light–matter coupling

Exciton–photon interaction in microcavities and heterostructures, from radiative decay and exchange interactions to strong-coupling phenomena.

Exciton–photon coupling · Optical response · Polaritons
